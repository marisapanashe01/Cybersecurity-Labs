# 🛡️ Cybersecurity Lab

### Understand the system. Examine the evidence. Strengthen the defence.

Security is more than knowing how attacks work. It involves understanding systems, analysing behaviour, investigating evidence, and learning how to identify weaknesses before they become bigger problems.

This repository is my central hub for practical cybersecurity exercises, network analysis, Linux exploration, and digital forensics as I continue my studies in Cybersecurity and Forensics.

## 📋 My Investigation Principles

- 🔍 **Observe before concluding.** Base findings on evidence, not assumptions.
- 🧾 **Document the process.** Record relevant steps, observations, and limitations.
- ⚖️ **Preserve evidence.** Follow appropriate evidence-handling procedures and maintain integrity.
- 🛡️ **Practise responsibly.** Work only on systems and data I own or am explicitly authorised to examine.
- 🧠 **Learn from every investigation.** Explain what the evidence supports and what remains uncertain.
